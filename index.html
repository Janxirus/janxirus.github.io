Shardlight Client

A standalone Minecraft: Java Edition launcher. Every setup lives in its own project with its own version, loader, mods and Java. It searches Modrinth and CurseForge, checks dependencies and conflicts before installing, explains crashes in plain words, and backs up worlds.
